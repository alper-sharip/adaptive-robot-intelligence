The one closed-form angle: the elbow

The 7th joint: elbow swivel

The other six angles have no single closed form: a pose fixes six numbers and the arm has seven joints, so the elbow can swing around the shoulder–wrist line without moving the gripper. Try the swivel slider: the gripper stays put while the arm moves.
